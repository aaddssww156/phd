# Development and External Validation of a Multimodal AI Mortality Prediction Model Using Multicenter Data

**Авторы:** Mamandipoor B., Hsu C.N., Krause M., Schmidt U.H., Gabriel R.A.

**Дата:** 2025-12-15 | **arXiv:** [2512.19716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19716) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.19716)

---

## Аннотация

Early prediction of in-hospital mortality in critically ill patients can aid clinicians. A multimodal DL model was developed on MIMIC-III, MIMIC-IV, eICU, and HiRID, featuring time series from the first 24 hours of ICU admission. The model predicts risk of subsequent inpatient mortality.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Образец multicenter external validation на 4 базах. Multimodal: structured + unstructured данные. Первые 24 часа — как у тебя.

---

## Методологические заметки

Multimodal DL, 4 databases, first 24h, time series + text
