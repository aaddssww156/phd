# Interpretable Machine Learning Model for Early Prediction of 30-Day Mortality in ICU Patients With Coexisting Hypertension and Atrial Fibrillation

**Авторы:** Chen S., Si Y., Fan J., Sun L., Placencia G., Pishgar E., Alaei K., Pishgar M.

**Дата:** 2025-06-18 | **arXiv:** [2506.15036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15036) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.15036)

---

## Аннотация

Hypertension and AF often coexist in critically ill patients. This study analyzed 1,301 adult ICU patients from MIMIC-IV. Data from the first 24 hours were extracted. After quality control, missing data imputation, and feature selection, 17 clinically relevant variables were retained.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Методологический близнец: MICE → feature selection → ML на данных первых 24 часов. Та же группа Pishgar.
