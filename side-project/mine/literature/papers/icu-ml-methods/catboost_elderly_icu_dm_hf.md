# Predicting Short-Term Mortality in Elderly ICU Patients with Diabetes and Heart Failure: A Distributional Inference Framework

**Авторы:** Fan J., Chen S., Sun L., Si Y., Pishgar E., Alaei K., Placencia G., Pishgar M.

---

## Аннотация

Elderly ICU patients with coexisting diabetes mellitus and heart failure experience markedly elevated short-term mortality. Using MIMIC-IV and 1,478 patients, 19 clinically significant variables were identified. CatBoost achieved the highest test AUROC (0.863). The DREAM algorithm generates posterior mortality risk distributions rather than point estimates. Interpretability via ablation and ALE analysis.

---

## Релевантность проекту

CatBoost — лучшая модель (подтверждает твой выбор). DREAM для uncertainty quantification. ALE plots — альтернатива SHAP dependency plots.

## Методологические заметки

Two-stage feature selection, DREAM posterior distributions, ALE analysis
