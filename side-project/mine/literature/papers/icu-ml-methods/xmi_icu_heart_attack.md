# XMI-ICU: Explainable Machine Learning Model for Pseudo-Dynamic Prediction of Mortality in the ICU for Heart Attack Patients

**Авторы:** Mesinovic M., Watkinson P., Zhu T.

**Дата:** 2023-05-10 | **arXiv:** [2305.06109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06109) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06109)

---

## Аннотация

Heart attack remain one of the greatest contributors to mortality. We use two retrospective cohorts extracted from eICU and MIMIC-IV to develop a pseudo-dynamic ML framework for mortality prediction in the ICU with interpretability and clinical risk analysis. The framework relying on XGBoost achieved AUCs of 91.0 for 6-hour prediction of mortality. Time-resolved Shapley values provide clinical insight.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Time-resolved SHAP — интерпретация с временной динамикой. Внешняя валидация eICU→MIMIC-IV. Можно адаптировать pseudo-dynamic подход.

## Методологические заметки
