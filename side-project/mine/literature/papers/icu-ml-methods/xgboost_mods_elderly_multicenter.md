# Interpretable Machine Learning Model for Early Prediction of Mortality in Elderly Patients with MODS: Multicenter Study

**Авторы:** Liu X., Hu P., Mao Z., Kuo P.C., Li P., Liu C., Hu J., Li D., Cao D., Mark R.G., Celi L.A., Zhang Z., Zhou F.

**Дата:** 2020-01-28 | **arXiv:** [2001.10977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10977) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.10977)

---

## Аннотация

Elderly patients with MODS have high risk of death. The performance of current scoring systems remains unsatisfactory. This study develops an interpretable and generalizable model using XGBoost with SHAP. MIMIC-III, eICU-CRD and PLAGH-S databases were employed for model generation and evaluation.

## Релевантность проекту

Multicenter валидация на 3 базах — золотой стандарт. Образец для масштабирования твоего проекта.
