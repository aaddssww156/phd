# Improving Risk Stratification in Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy: A Novel Score Combining Echocardiography, Clinical, and Medication Data

**Авторы:** Taconne M., Corino V.D.A., Del Franco A., Giovani S., Olivotto I., Al Wazzan A., Donal E., Cerveri P., Mainardi L.

## Аннотация

HCM requires accurate risk stratification for ICD therapy decisions. Current ESC score exhibits moderate discriminative performance. We develop an explainable ML risk score leveraging echocardiographic, clinical, and medication data to predict 5-year composite cardiovascular outcome.
