# Tabular Foundation Models for Clinical Survival Analysis via Survival-Aware Adaptation

## Аннотация

Predicting time-to-event outcomes is fundamental in clinical decision-making. Recent tabular foundation models offer general-purpose representations for structured data. However, applicability to censored time-to-event prediction in clinical settings remains underexplored. We propose survival-aware adaptation.
