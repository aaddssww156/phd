## Аннотация

ML-based T2D risk prediction models lose effectiveness in real-world applications due to deficient external testing and fairness assessment. We developed a multi-dimensional framework evaluating discrimination, calibration, interpretability, and algorithmic fairness. XGBoost on NHANES (n=15,685) with 8 non-laboratory predictors.
