# Disentangling Shared and Task-Specific Representations from Multi-Modal Clinical Data

**Авторы:** Lyu H., Zeng H., Wang J., Yang H., He L., Chen Y., Li Z., Maier A., Bayer S., Song H.

**Дата:** 2026-05-05 | **arXiv:** [2605.03570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03570) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03570)

---

## Аннотация

Real-world clinical data is inherently multimodal. We propose a multi-task framework built on a unified Transformer backbone that disentangles shared and task-specific representations. Hard parameter sharing can trigger negative transfer when task gradients conflict.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Если у тебя multiple outcomes (КШ + смерть + ОПП) — multi-task с disentangled representations.

---

## Методологические заметки

Unified Transformer, shared + task-specific disentanglement, multi-task clinical prediction
