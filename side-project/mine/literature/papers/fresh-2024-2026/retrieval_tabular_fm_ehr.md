# Retrieval-aligned Tabular Foundation Models Enable Robust Clinical Risk Prediction in EHR Under Real-world Constraints

**Авторы:** Pham M.K., Nguyen Ho T.L., Dao T.T.P., Mai T.T., Tran M.T., Ward M.E., Geary U., Brennan R., McDonald N., Crane M., Bezbradica M.

**Дата:** 2026-04-02 | **arXiv:** [2604.01841](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01841) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.01841)

---

## Аннотация

Clinical prediction from structured EHRs is challenging due to high dimensionality, heterogeneity, class imbalance, and distribution shift. We present a multi-cohort EHR benchmark comparing classical, deep tabular, and TICL models. PFN-based TICL models are sample-efficient in low-data regimes but degrade under class imbalance and distribution shift.

---

## Релевантность проекту

ВАЖНО: TICL модели деградируют при class imbalance. Классические методы (твой CatBoost/XGBoost) надёжнее в условиях дисбаланса.

---

## Методологические заметки

Multi-cohort benchmark, classical vs TICL vs deep tabular, cross-cohort generalization
