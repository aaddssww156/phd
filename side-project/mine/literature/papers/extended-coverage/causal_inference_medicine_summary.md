## Аннотация

Healthcare practitioners are not content with mere predictions — they are also interested in the cause-effect relation between input features and clinical outcomes. Understanding such relations will help inform interventions and policy decisions beyond what prediction alone can offer.

## Релевантность проекту

SHAP показывает association, но не causation. SII повышен → риск КШ? Или КШ вызывает SII? Causal inference различает.
