# Inadequacy of Common Stochastic Neural Networks for Reliable Clinical Decision Support

**Авторы:** Lindenmeyer A., Blattmann M., Franke S., Neumuth T., Schneider D.

**Дата:** 2024-01-24 | **arXiv:** [2401.13657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13657) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.13657)

---

## Аннотация

Common DL approaches have the tendency towards overconfidence under data shift. Such inappropriate extrapolation may have dire consequences. This highlights the importance of reliable estimation of local uncertainty. While stochastic NNs have been heralded as a potential solution, we show they are inadequate for clinical settings.

## Релевантность проекту

КРИТИЧНО: стандартные stochastic NN (MC Dropout, ensembles) недостаточно надёжны. Conformal prediction даёт более строгие гарантии.
