# Domain-invariant Clinical Representation Learning by Bridging Data Distribution Shift across EMR Datasets

**Авторы:** Zhang Z., Wang Y., Zhu Y., Ma X., Wang Y., Gao J., Ma L., Tang W., Zhang X., Wang L.

**Дата:** 2023-10-11 | **arXiv:** [2310.07799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07799) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.07799)

## Аннотация

Emerging diseases present challenges in symptom recognition. An effective prognostic model could assist physicians. We propose domain-invariant clinical representation learning to bridge data distribution shifts across different EMR datasets.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Модель, устойчивая к смене датасета — ключевое требование для clinical deployment.
