# Multimodal Synthesis of MRI and Tabular Data with Diffusion in a Joint Latent Space via Cross-Attention

**Авторы:** Mensing D., Kapar J., Hirsch J.G., Gunther M., Hahn H., Wright M.N.

**Дата:** 2026-05-05 | **arXiv:** [2605.06699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06699) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06699)

---

## Аннотация

We propose a multimodal latent diffusion model that jointly synthesizes volumetric MRI and tabular clinical data within a shared latent space via cross-attention. This enables coherent generation across modalities.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Cross-attention для fusion таблиц и другой модальности — паттерн, воспроизводимый для таблиц + временных рядов.
