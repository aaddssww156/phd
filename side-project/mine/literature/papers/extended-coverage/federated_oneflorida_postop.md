# Federated Learning with Multi-Partner OneFlorida+ Consortium Data for Predicting Major Postoperative Complications

**Авторы:** Ren Y., Vemuri V.S., Hu Z., Shickel B., Guan Z., Loftus T.J., Rashidi P., Ozrazgat-Baslanti T., Bihorac A.

**Дата:** 2026-03-17 | **arXiv:** [2603.16723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16723) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.16723)

---

## Аннотация

We develop and validate federated learning models for predicting major postoperative complications and mortality using 358,644 patients, 494,163 procedures from 5 healthcare institutions (OneFlorida Data Trust). FL models offer robust generalizability while preserving data privacy and security.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Реальный multicenter FL на сотнях тысяч пациентов — production-grade подход к масштабированию.

---

## Методологические заметки

5 hospitals, 358K patients, internal + external validation, FL vs centralized comparison
