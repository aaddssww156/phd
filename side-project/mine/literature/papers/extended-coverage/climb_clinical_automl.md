## Аннотация

Despite significant promise, real-world AI applications remain limited due to the domain expert-AI conundrum: clinician scientists face barriers in accessing SOTA tools. CliMB is an AI partner combining AutoML with clinical workflow integration, interpretability, and rigorous evaluation.

## Релевантность проекту

van der Schaar — ведущий исследователь ML for healthcare. CliMB — видение того, как prediction models должны создаваться врачами.
