## Аннотация

Early and accurate prediction of in-hospital mortality within the first 24 hours of ICU admission is crucial for timely clinical interventions. Traditional scoring systems have limitations in accuracy and adaptability. This review evaluates innovative methodologies leveraging first-day ICU data, including ML, novel biomarkers, and integration approaches.
