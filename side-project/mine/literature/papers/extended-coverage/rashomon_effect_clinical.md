## Аннотация

The coexistence of multiple models with comparable performance (Rashomon Effect) poses fundamental challenges for trustworthy deployment. Small, imbalanced, noisy datasets coupled with high-dimensional clinical features amplify this multiplicity. We propose a capacity-aware model selection framework with perturbation validation.
