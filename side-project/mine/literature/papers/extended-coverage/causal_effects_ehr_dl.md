# Incorporating Causal Effects into Deep Learning Predictions on EHR Data

**Авторы:** Li J., Yang H., Jia X., Kumar V., Steinbach M., Simon G.

**Дата:** 2020-11-11 | **arXiv:** [2011.05466](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05466) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.05466)

---

## Аннотация

EHR data analysis plays a crucial role in healthcare. Because of highly complex underlying causality, causal inference on EHR is challenging. We propose a method to quantify clinically well-defined causal effects as a generalized estimation vector and incorporate it into DL models.
