# Uncertainty-Calibrated Prediction of Randomly-Timed Biomarker Trajectories with Conformal Bands

**Авторы:** Tassopoulou V., Stamouli C., Shou H., Pappas G.J., Davatzikos C.

**Дата:** 2025-11-17 | **arXiv:** [2511.13911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13911) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.13911)

---

## Аннотация

We introduce a conformal method for uncertainty-calibrated prediction of biomarker trajectories resulting from randomly-timed clinical visits. Our approach extends conformal prediction via a novel nonconformity score that produces prediction bands guaranteed to cover the unknown trajectory.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Если добавишь динамику биомаркеров (ЧСС, SpO2 во времени) — этот метод даёт prediction bands вместо точечных прогнозов.
