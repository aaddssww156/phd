## Аннотация

Black-box ML models are now routinely used in high-risk settings like medical diagnostics, which demand uncertainty quantification. Conformal prediction is a user-friendly paradigm for creating statistically rigorous uncertainty sets/intervals. The sets are valid in a distribution-free sense with explicit, non-asymptotic guarantees. Works with any pre-trained model.
