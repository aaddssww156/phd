# Deep Bayesian Gaussian Processes for Uncertainty Estimation in Electronic Health Records

**Авторы:** Li Y., Rao S., Hassaine A., Ramakrishnan R., Zhu Y., Canoy D., Salimi-Khorshidi G., Lukasiewicz T., Rahimi K.

**Дата:** 2020-03-23 | **arXiv:** [2003.10170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.10170) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.10170)

---

## Аннотация

One major impediment to wider use of DL for clinical decision making is the difficulty of assigning a level of confidence to model predictions. Deep Bayesian Gaussian Processes offer more expressiveness than Bayesian NN while capturing interpretable uncertainty across multiple levels.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Альтернатива conformal prediction для uncertainty quantification — более выразительная, но сложнее в имплементации.

---

## Методологические заметки

Deep Bayesian GP, uncertainty decomposition, UK Biobank validation
