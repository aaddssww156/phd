# MIND: Modality-Informed Knowledge Distillation Framework for Multimodal Clinical Prediction Tasks

**Авторы:** Guerra-Manzanares A., Shamout F.E.

## Аннотация

Multimodal datasets in medical settings are typically smaller than unimodal counterparts. The increase in modalities is often associated with an increase in multimodal network size, which may be undesirable. MIND proposes modality-informed knowledge distillation: large multimodal teacher → compact model for deployment.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Важно: если добавишь временные ряды к таблицам, модель станет больше → риск переобучения. MIND предлагает дистилляцию как решение.
