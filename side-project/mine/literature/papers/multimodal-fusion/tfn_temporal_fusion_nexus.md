# Temporal Fusion Nexus: Task-Agnostic Multi-Modal Embedding Model for Irregular Time Series and Clinical Narratives

**Авторы:** Kumar A., Rauch S., Cypko M., Naik M., Schapranow M.P., Rashid A., Halleck F., Osmanodja B., Roller R., Pape L., Budde K., Schiffer M., Amft O.

## Аннотация

TFN integrates irregular time series and unstructured clinical narratives. In post-kidney transplant care (3,382 patients), TFN achieved AUC 0.96 for graft loss, 0.84 for rejection, 0.86 for mortality. TFN outperformed unimodal baselines: +10% AUC over time-series only, +5% over time-series + static data.
