# RAIM: Recurrent Attentive and Intensive Model of Multimodal Patient Monitoring Data

**Авторы:** Xu Y., Biswal S., Deshpande S.R., Maher K.O., Sun J.

**Дата:** 2018-07-23 | **arXiv:** [1807.08820](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08820) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.08820)

---

## Аннотация

With vast amounts of continuous patient monitoring data (ECG, real-time vital signs, medications) becoming available, integration of high-density monitoring data with discrete clinical events (diagnosis, medications, labs) is challenging but potentially rewarding. RAIM uses RNN + attention for multimodal ICU data.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Классический архитектурный паттерн RNN+attention для мультимодальных ICU данных. Всё ещё актуален как baseline.
