## Аннотация

LLMs and multimodal foundation models are enabling medical AI to move beyond isolated prediction to multistep clinical tasks requiring planning, tool use, memory, iterative correction, and coordination. Scoping review with systematic evidence mapping across 1,649 screened records.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Куда движется медицинский AI: от prediction models → agentic systems. Для раздела Future Directions.
