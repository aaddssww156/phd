# Monitoring Fairness in Machine Learning Models that Predict Patient Mortality in the ICU

**Авторы:** van Schaik T.A., Liu X., Atallah L., Badawi O.

## Аннотация

This work proposes a fairness monitoring approach for ML models predicting patient mortality in the ICU. We investigate performance across race, sex, and medical diagnoses. Documentation bias in clinical measurement provides more detailed and insightful comparison than traditional accuracy metrics alone.
