# Improving Fairness of Large Language Model-Based ICU Mortality Prediction via Case-Based Prompting

**Авторы:** Zhang G., Long Y., Zhou Y., Zhang Y., Hong S.

## Аннотация

LLMs show strong potential in structured medical prediction tasks, but outputs may exhibit biases related to sex, age, and race. We propose case-based prompting for debiasing without degrading predictive performance.
