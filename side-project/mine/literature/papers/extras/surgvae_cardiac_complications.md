# A Novel Generative Multi-Task Representation Learning Approach for Predicting Postoperative Complications in Cardiac Surgery Patients

**Авторы:** Shen J., Xue B., Kannampallil T., Lu C., Abraham J.

**Дата:** 2024-12-02 | **arXiv:** [2412.01950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01950) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.01950)

## Аннотация

We developed surgVAE, uncovering intrinsic patterns via cross-task and cross-cohort representation learning. Retrospective cohort: adult surgical patients over 4 years. Predicts 6 key postoperative complications.
