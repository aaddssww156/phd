## Аннотация

We offer a pragmatic model to operationalize responsible, secure, and sustainable healthcare AI with five pillars: Leadership & Strategy, MLOps & Technical Infrastructure, Governance & Ethics, Education & Workforce Development, and Change Management & Adoption.
