# Variational Disentanglement for Rare Event Modeling

**Авторы:** Xiu Z., Tao C., Gao M., Davis C., Goldstein B.A., Henao R.

**Дата:** 2020-09-17 | **arXiv:** [2009.08541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.08541) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.08541)

---

## Аннотация

In healthcare risk prediction, the proportion of cases with the condition of interest is often very low. We propose a variational disentanglement approach to semi-parametrically learn separate representations for rare event classification, avoiding class_weight correction.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Альтернатива class_weight='balanced': disentangled representation вместо коррекции весов. Может дать лучшую калибровку.

## Методологические заметки

Variational disentanglement, semi-parametric, separate representations
