# Beyond Random Missingness: Clinically Rethinking for Healthcare Time Series Imputation

**Авторы:** Qian L., Yang Y., Du W., Wang J., Dobson R., Ibrahim Z.

**Дата:** 2024-05-26 | **arXiv:** [2405.17508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17508) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.17508)

---

## Аннотация

This study investigates the impact of masking strategies on time series imputation models in healthcare. Random masking fails to capture structured missing patterns in clinical data. Using PhysioNet Challenge 2012, we analyse 11 imputation methods. Masking choices significantly influence model performance. Imputation accuracy doesn't necessarily translate to optimal clinical prediction.

## Релевантность проекту

Клинические пропуски НЕ случайны (MNAR): SpO2 не измеряют у стабильных пациентов, тесты назначают при подозрении на ухудшение. Random masking evaluation невалидна.
