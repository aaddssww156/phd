# An Improved Tabular Data Generator with VAE-GMM Integration

**Авторы:** Apellaniz P.A., Parras J., Zazo S.

**Дата:** 2024-04-12 | **arXiv:** [2404.08434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08434) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.08434)

---

## Аннотация

Current approaches like CTGAN struggle with complex tabular structures. We propose a VAE-based model incorporating Bayesian Gaussian Mixture (BGM) within the VAE architecture, avoiding strictly Gaussian latent space assumptions. Outperforms CTGAN and TVAE on medical datasets.
