# Time-Dependent Iterative Imputation for Multivariate Longitudinal Clinical Data

**Авторы:** Noy O., Shamir R.

**Дата:** 2023-04-16 | **arXiv:** [2304.07821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07821) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.07821)

---

## Аннотация

We developed TDI, integrating forward-filling and Iterative Imputer with dynamic weighting based on clinical patterns (missing rates, measurement frequency). On MIMIC-III (>500,000 observations), TDI outperformed SOTA for 25/30 clinical variables with RMSE 0.63 vs 0.85 for SoftImpute. TDI imputation led to improved risk prediction.

---

## Релевантность проекту

Если добавишь лонгитюдные данные, TDI лучше MICE для временных рядов. Сравни со своим MICE-подходом.

---

## Методологические заметки

Dynamic weighting, forward-filling + Iterative Imputer, missing rate patterns, RMSE 0.63
