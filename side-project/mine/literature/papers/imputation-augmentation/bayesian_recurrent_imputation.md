# Bayesian Recurrent Framework for Missing Data Imputation and Prediction with Clinical Time Series

**Авторы:** Guo Y., Liu Z., Krishnswamy P., Ramasamy S.

**Дата:** 2019-11-18 | **arXiv:** [1911.07572](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.07572) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.07572)

---

## Аннотация

Real-world clinical time series exhibit high prevalence of missing values. Traditional approaches decouple imputation from prediction. We introduce a unified Bayesian recurrent framework for simultaneous imputation and prediction. Strong performance gains over SOTA on MIMIC-III and PhysioNet.

---

## Релевантность проекту

MICE даёт детерминированные импутации. Bayesian framework даёт распределение → оценка неопределённости импутации.
