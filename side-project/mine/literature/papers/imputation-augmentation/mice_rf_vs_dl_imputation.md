# Missing Data Imputation for Noisy Time-Series Data and Applications in Healthcare

**Авторы:** Le L.P., Nguyen Thi X.H., Nguyen T., Riegler M.A., Halvorsen P., Nguyen B.T.

**Дата:** 2024-12-15 | **arXiv:** [2412.11164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11164) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.11164)

---

## Аннотация

We compare imputation methods including MICE-RF and advanced DL approaches (SAITS, BRITS, Transformer) for noisy, missing time series data across 10-80% missing rates. MICE-RF can effectively impute missing data compared to DL methods. Imputation can have denoising effects.
