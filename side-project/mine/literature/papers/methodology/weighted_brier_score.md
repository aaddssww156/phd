## Аннотация

The classic Brier score is a popular measure of prediction accuracy; however, it is insufficient for effectively assessing clinical utility. We propose a class of weighted Brier scores that aligns with the decision-theoretic framework of clinical utility. We decompose the weighted Brier score into discrimination and calibration components and link it to the H-measure.
