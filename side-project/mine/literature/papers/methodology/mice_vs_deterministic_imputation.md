## Аннотация

Methods to handle missing data have been extensively explored. However, in the context of clinical risk prediction models, deterministic imputation is better suited since the outcome is not included in the imputation model and the imputation method can be easily applied to future patients. We provide a tutorial on bootstrapping followed by deterministic imputation.

## Релевантность проекту

MICE (твой подход) vs детерминированная импутация. Аргумент: для prediction лучше детерминированная импутация (легче в production). Сравни.
