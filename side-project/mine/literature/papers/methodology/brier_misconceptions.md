## Аннотация

The Brier score is a widely used metric evaluating overall performance of probabilistic predictions for binary outcomes in clinical research. However, its interpretation can be complex, as it does not align with commonly taught concepts in medical statistics. We explore prevalent misconceptions surrounding the Brier score.
