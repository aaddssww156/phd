# TRACE: A Temporal Conditional Estimation for Multimodal Time Series Foundation Models

## Аннотация

Time series foundation models aim to learn generalizable temporal representations. In real-world multimodal settings, time series are affected by temporal misalignment and partial modality missingness. TRACE proposes a conditional estimation paradigm that accounts for cross-modal dependencies, unlike naive imputation or masking strategies.
