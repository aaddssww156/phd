# Multimodal Deep Learning for Early Prediction of Patient Deterioration in the ICU

**Авторы:** Sadanandan B.

**Дата:** 2026-03-16 | **arXiv:** [2603.14719](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14719) | **PDF:** [Скачать](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.14719)

---

## Аннотация

We present a multimodal DL approach combining structured time-series data (vital signs, labs) with unstructured clinical notes to predict patient deterioration within 24 hours. Using MIMIC-IV, 74,822 ICU stays and 5.7 million hourly prediction samples. Architecture: BiLSTM encoder for temporal data + ClinicalBERT for text → late fusion.

## Релевантность проекту

Масштабный подход: 5.7M сэмплов, BiLSTM + трансформер. Прямой аналог для добавления временной компоненты к табличным данным.
